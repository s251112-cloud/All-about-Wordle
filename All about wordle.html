All About Wordle

The Ultimate Word Game Hub 🟩🟨⬛

1. What is Wordle?

Wordle is a popular web-based word puzzle game created by programmer Josh Wardle. Players have six attempts to guess a secret 5-letter word. It's a brilliant mix of logic, language skills, and daily problem-solving!

2. How to Read the Colours

Every time you make a guess, the colour of the tiles changes to show you how close you are:

W
Green: The letter is correct and in the **exact right spot**!
O
Yellow: The letter is in the word, but in a **different spot**.
R
Gray: The letter is **not in the word** at all.

3. Best Pro-Strategies

  • Pick a Strong Starter Word: Use words packed with vowels and common letters (like SLATE, STARE, TRACE, or CRANE).
  • Don't Reuse Gray Letters: If a letter turns grey, avoid putting it in your next guesses to unlock missing hints!
