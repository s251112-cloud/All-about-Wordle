## 🧠 6. Wordle Strategy & Statistical Insights

To consistently solve the puzzle within the six-try limit, players can utilize phonetic frequency and strategic elimination tracking.

### 🎯 A. The Optimal Starting Words
Statistically, the best starting words contain a high concentration of common English vowels (A, E, O) and top-tier consonants (R, S, T). 
*   **CRANE / SLATE:** Highly recommended by computer algorithms to eliminate common layout structures early.
    *   *Example:* The girl always inputs **CRANE** as her opening word to quickly find the vowels.

### 🐾 B. The Trapped Word Danger (The "_ATCH" or "_IGHT" Trap)
One of the biggest pitfalls in Wordle is falling into a "rhyme trap," where you have four correct letters but too many possible starting consonants remaining.
*   **The Scenario:** You discover `_ I G H T`. The correct word could be *MIGHT*, *LIGHT*, *SIGHT*, *NIGHT*, or *RIGHT*.
*   **The Strategy (Elimination Rows):** Instead of guessing those words one by one and losing your turns, sacrifice one row to input a completely different word that packs all those missing consonants together!
    *   *Example:* If a girl is trapped on `_ I G H T`, she can guess **FLOWN**. This single guess checks *F*, *L*, *W* and *N* all at once, protecting her win streak from running out of tries!
